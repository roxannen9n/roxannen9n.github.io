Master's thesis · Optimization · Ongoing

Multi-objective Mixed-Integer Linear Programming for Electric Vehicle Charging Station Location in Ho Chi Minh City

My current thesis focuses on mixed-integer linear programming for electric vehicle charging station location in Ho Chi Minh City considering multiple objectives such as investor's profit optimization, traffic accessibility and electricity network.

01 · Optimization

EV charging station location and sizing optimization ↗

Where should new charging stations be built, and which charger configurations should they use? A profit-driven network expansion model for Ho Chi Minh City, with processed GIS inputs and reproducible optimization results.

Result: 63 new stations selected, serving 3,498 additional charging sessions/day with 119.77 million VND/day in modeled incremental profit.

Method
GIS-based demand allocation · Mixed-integer linear programming
Objective
Maximize incremental daily network profit after electricity, installation and land costs.
View project on GitHub ↗

02 · Optimization

Shade for the walk to Thao Dien Metro Station in HCMC ↗

Where could a limited number of new trees provide more shade? A spatial optimization study of tree-planting locations along walking routes to Thao Dien metro station.

Result: 10 larger-crown trees selected in the reference scenario, reducing mean modeled sun exposure by 14.6 seconds per potential one-way access trip.

Method
GIS-based shade estimation · MILP
Objective
Maximize population-weighted reductions in sun exposure within a tree-count budget.
View project on GitHub ↗

03 · Forecasting

EV charging demand forecasting ↗

Workshop Poster at UEH Applied Mathematics Workshop 2026

A comparison of statistical, temporal, spatial, and spatio-temporal models for hourly charging-station occupancy.

Result: LSTM performed best among four models across 275 stations, with a validation RMSE of 3.67.

Method
ARIMA · LSTM · GCN · GCN-LSTM
Objective
Compare forecasting performance and the contribution of temporal and spatial dependencies.
View project on GitHub ↗

04 · Reinforcement learning

EV charging scheduling with Deep Q-Network ↗

Presented & In Proceedings of ELG 2026 · International Conference on Economics, Law and Government

A DQN framework for scheduling charging under uncertain electricity prices and driving demand.

Result: 68.7% lower mean electricity cost per charging step than the naive benchmark in the reported simulation.

Method
Deep reinforcement learning · DQN
Objective
Reduce charging costs while meeting future driving demand.
