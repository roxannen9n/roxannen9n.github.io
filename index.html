I study how mathematical models can support decisions about cities and transport.

I'm a Master of Mathematical Economics student at the University of Economics Ho Chi Minh City (UEH). My interests include smart cities, urban planning, optimization, optimal control, and operations research.

My projects explore electric vehicle charging and pedestrian access to metro stations, using mathematical optimization, reinforcement learning, forecasting, and spatial data.

Master's thesis · In progress

Where should EV charging stations go?

My current thesis focuses on mixed-integer linear programming for electric vehicle charging station location in Ho Chi Minh City.

I am interested in how operations research can help plan infrastructure under limited resources, and how data can inform these decisions.

01 · Reinforcement learning

EV charging scheduling with Deep Q-Network ↗

When should an electric vehicle charge? A DQN framework for scheduling charging under uncertain electricity prices and driving demand.

Method
Deep reinforcement learning · DQN
Objective
Reduce charging costs while meeting future driving demand.

ELG 2026 · International Conference on Economics, Law and Government

02 · Forecasting

EV charging demand forecasting ↗

How much do spatial relationships help predict charging demand? A comparison of statistical, temporal, spatial, and spatio-temporal models for hourly charging-station occupancy.

Method
ARIMA · LSTM · GCN · GCN-LSTM
Objective
Compare forecasting performance and the contribution of temporal and spatial dependencies.

Workshop poster · UEH Applied Mathematics Workshop 2026

View project on GitHub ↗

03 · Spatial optimization

Shade for the walk to Thao Dien metro station ↗

Where could a limited number of new trees provide more shade? A spatial optimization study of tree-planting locations along walking routes to Thao Dien metro station.

Method
GIS-based shade estimation · MILP
Objective
Maximize population-weighted reductions in sun exposure within a tree-count budget.
